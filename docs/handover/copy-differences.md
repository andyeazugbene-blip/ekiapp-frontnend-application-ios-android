# Client copy not used word for word

Generated by string match against the Community Buy and Regular Delivery documents, on 2026-11-06. "Regular Delivery" counts as "Foodstuff Subscription", the name Phase 1 and Handbook 9 use.

A line here doesn't always mean something is missing. There are four common reasons:

1. **The app shows that state in a shared card**, with different wording, not on a screen of its own.
2. **The line would be untrue in pledge mode.** "Pay securely through Eki" and refund lines assume payment at joining, but the UK charges later. Those screens keep the true wording until the UK moves to card holds.
3. **The screen belongs to the older organiser flow**, which supplier offers replace once `communityBuyOffersRequired` is on.
4. **The line is an instruction to the developer**, such as "Do not show fake subscriber, renewal or revenue figures", not text for the screen.
